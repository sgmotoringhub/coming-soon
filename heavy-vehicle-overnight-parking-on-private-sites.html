Heavy Vehicle Overnight Parking on Private Sites: What Owners Need to Do

Heavy vehicles can now park overnight at some private, non-residential sites. You need consent and a free registration.

Regulations · Updated 23 Nov 2026

On 14 September 2026, LTA announced that heavy vehicles can park overnight at certain private sites from 15 September 2026.

Which vehicles?

  • Goods vehicles and concrete mixers with a maximum laden weight above 5,000kg.
  • Buses with more than 15 seats, excluding the driver.

Where can they park?

Private, non-residential premises, such as industrial facilities, authorised work sites and commercial properties, with the facility owner's consent. They still cannot park on public roads or in residential estates.

How do you register?

  1. Register the parking location through the OneMotoring Digital Service.
  2. Submit proof of consent from the premises owner.
  3. Wait for LTA's letter of acknowledgement.

There is no fee, and each lodgement is valid for up to two years. LTA can revoke it if requirements are not met, and said it will step up enforcement against parking on public roads.

Tip: Get the site owner's consent in writing, and diarise the two-year renewal.
